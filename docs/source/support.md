# Support and project sustainability

HomiCSx is maintained as citable research software, with emphasis on a tested,
reproducible core rather than a continuous feature roadmap. Major correctness,
reproducibility, and compatibility reports are welcome, but response and release
timelines cannot be guaranteed.

Use the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/AmirTahouni/HomiCSx/issues)
for reproducible bug reports and focused proposals. Include the version or
commit, environment, minimal example, and complete error output.

The experimental stochastic and visualization modules are outside the
publication-supported API. The MIT License permits continued use, adaptation,
redistribution, and community forks if maintainer availability changes.

See also the repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SUPPORT.md`, and `SECURITY.md`.
